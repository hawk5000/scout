# Product and interface

## Public site

Lead with: “Know what’s happening outside your hotel’s four walls.” Explain companies, projects, and events that could bring business, an initial blueprint, and twice-weekly updates. Show a clearly labeled sample deliverable, three scout cards, assisted setup steps, working pricing, and a short get-started form. Do not claim customer adoption, verified ROI, complete coverage, or live findings using fixtures. Do not show nonfunctional agents as available.

## Customer workspace

Use warm white content, forest-green sidebar, near-black text, restrained gold accents, readable tables, and responsive cards. Give findings the screen space. Use accessible labels, keyboard controls, visible focus, and text status indicators.

Navigation:
- Overview: hotel and research area, last successful update, next scheduled cycle; new findings, material changes, and verification-needed items.
- Market Blueprint: searchable/filterable accumulated companies, projects, and events; full record details and version history.
- Research Agents: three real agents sharing the blueprint and schedule.
- Updates: dated cycle summaries, new/changed records, and coverage limitations.
- Exports: selection/filter-based downloads and readable briefs.
- Hotel Profile: confirmed facts, market boundaries, priorities, exclusions, notification recipients.

Finding cards: name, what happened, why it may matter, location/distance when calculated, relevant dates, organizations, source links, last checked/verified, what changed. Actions: View details, Save, Dismiss, Copy, Export. Keep Save/Dismiss personal or property-private according to the existing access model. They are not CRM stages.

## Agent UI

Show name/responsibility, coverage, last successful run, next cycle, results count, and truthful status. Use Scheduled, Researching, Completed, Partial, Needs attention, or Paused as appropriate. A failed or budget-stopped scan must not appear Completed.

Agent detail tabs: Findings, Coverage, Run history. Allow viewing findings, editing supported coverage, and pausing scheduled research. Viewing a card or tab must never trigger paid research. Keep manual rescan admin-controlled and budgeted unless the user approves a customer allowance. Explain when coverage changes take effect without silently launching an unbounded new baseline.

Show “No meaningful changes found” after a successful quiet cycle, “No findings match these filters” for empty search results, and separate initial setup, paused, stale, partial, and failure states. Do not invent fresh findings to keep a dashboard busy. Keep chat out of the initial release.

## Assisted setup and administration

Guide the operator through website/address, draft public hotel profile, human confirmation, boundaries and categories, exclusions, customer access, recipients, schedule, baseline generation, review, and handoff. Treat roughly one hour of hands-on setup as a target to measure, not a guarantee. Avoid gathering unnecessary private hotel data.

Admin controls: activation/entitlements, setup status, source and scan failures, per-hotel usage/spend, baseline versus ongoing cost, retry controls, and evidence review. Reuse SIL identity and enablement when applicable to the target build; inspect actual contracts first. Payment stays outside SIL and no Stripe flow is implied by a pricing page. Do not implement new credential handling based solely on historical notes.
