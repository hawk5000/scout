# Product specification

Brand: SCOUT by SalesHospitality
Tagline: Your hotel’s market research assistant.
Owner: Parrie Bunnell / SalesHospitality.

## Buyer and job
Serve an existing hotel sales team or GM who researches the surrounding market. Remove discovery, verification, aggregation and duplicate checking. The hotel controls strategy and moves the intelligence into its own CRM, spreadsheet, Word document or outreach tools.

## Launch capabilities
- Corporate SCOUT: employers, local facilities, expansions and activities potentially supporting recurring business travel.
- Project SCOUT: construction, infrastructure, renovations, installations, contractors and temporary project activity.
- Event SCOUT: tournaments, conferences, competitions, festivals and training gatherings with potential overnight demand.

One shared blueprint; two market update cycles weekly. Review baseline first, then update records and alert only on meaningful changes. Rechecking sources remains necessary. No fixed number of daily leads or guaranteed bookings. Include verified public contacts where available without making them mandatory for every useful market finding.

## Commercial working assumptions
$99 assisted setup plus $50 monthly per hotel, all three scouts included. Roughly one hour hands-on setup is a target to measure. Initial and recurring API estimates are hypotheses; no guaranteed profit. Sharing across nearby hotels may reduce public research cost, but private customer data must remain isolated.

## Initial exclusions
CRM/pipeline, automated outreach, task assignment, sales coaching, PMS integration, voice, payment processing and unrestricted research chat. A broad agent marketplace and additional agents are future decisions.

## Pilot
Seattle and Portland are intended distinct markets. Specific hotels and property boundaries are not yet provided. Do not guess them or run live research solely because these cities appear here.
