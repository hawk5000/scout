# Product scope
## Goal
Make SCOUT a hotel intelligence workspace with four uses: discover market opportunities, understand the named comp set, see public mentions of the hotel, and receive a fresh action brief. More frequent scans alone do not solve quiet-market subscription value.

## Existing foundation
Retain Corporate SCOUT, Project SCOUT and Event SCOUT, their shared market blueprint, evidence/history, saved/dismissed states and exports. Account moves and changes stay within existing corporate coverage. Preserve the existing twice-weekly research model; do not increase cadence merely to produce content.

## New capabilities
- Competitor SCOUT (working name): public noteworthy changes at the hotel's explicitly selected comp set.
- Property SCOUT (working name): public mentions of the subscribing hotel, including positive, negative and neutral context.
- SCOUT Briefing (working name): refresh relevant research and synthesize the latest findings across all five research categories into one action brief.
These are five research agents plus one synthesizing briefing agent; the original three remain separately accessible.

## Competitor scope
Events hosted/promoted, packages/offers, renovations, openings, facilities/amenity changes, and material service/positioning changes. Use only selected competitors; do not silently expand to other nearby hotels. This does not authorize a rate shopper, parity engine, occupancy estimate, or access to private competitor information.

## Property scope
News, reviews, event listings, articles and accessible public web pages mentioning the correct property. Monitor mentions; do not post review responses. Coverage is source-dependent and must be stated honestly.

## Briefing scope
Include practical implications, connections, and suggested priorities from evidence. This is an intentional expansion from passive research into an interpreted brief. Do not create assigned tasks, CRM stages, outreach messages/cadences, or send anything. The user takes the brief into existing workflows.

## Commercial assumptions
The discussion considered $50–$60/month as a subscription-value question, not an approved price change. Keep current pricing until explicitly changed. No claims of saving several days weekly, exhaustive monitoring, verified demand, or proven margins without evidence. Marketplace comparison remains unperformed.

## Exclusions
No PMS, booking, guest data, voice, CRM rebuild, separate top-account monitoring, road/airport disruption agent, automated outreach, review-response tool, Stripe, rate parity, or unrestricted general chat. Preserve current branding; logo cleanup is not part of this expansion.
